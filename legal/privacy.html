Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

The short version

Néno collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no tracking and no advertising, and the app sends nothing to a server. Everything you learn stays on your device.

What the app stores

Néno keeps your progress on your device only, in the app's own database (IndexedDB) and in its settings:

  • which cards you have answered and when each one is due again,
  • your settings: language, daily amount, voice, reminder time, theme,
  • which themed decks you own.

None of this leaves your device. It is not transmitted, not backed up to us and not analysed. We cannot see it.

What the app does not do

  • No user account, no sign-in, no email address.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no advertising identifiers.
  • No sharing with third parties.
  • No connection to our servers. We do not run any.

The app loads only files that ship inside it.

Purchases

Themed decks are sold through the App Store, and Apple handles the purchase. We never receive your payment details: they stay with Apple, and Apple's own privacy policy applies to them. The app asks your device which products you own and stores that list locally, so your decks work offline. Apple gives us sales reports in aggregated form, from which no individual person can be identified.

Reminders

If you set a daily reminder, the app schedules a local notification on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere and nothing is recorded, and you can withdraw the permission at any time in the iOS settings.

Crash reports

If you have agreed, under Privacy and Analytics in the iOS settings, to share data with app developers, Apple may give us aggregated crash and usage statistics. That is a setting of Apple's rather than one of the app's, and you can change it there whenever you like.

If you write to us

If you write to kontakt@nenoapp.eu, we process what is in your message: your email address, your name if you give it, and the content itself. We need that to answer you, and for nothing else. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR where it concerns a purchase or your use of the app, and Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR for anything else: the legitimate interest in answering enquiries. The address is a forward, through our domain registrar, into a mailbox we read ourselves. Nothing is passed on. We delete the correspondence once it is settled and no legal retention period applies; for commercial email that can be up to six years under § 257 HGB. The app itself never sends us anything. Everything we know about you, you wrote to us.

Deleting your data

Two ways, and both take effect immediately:

  • In the app: Settings, then Reset progress.
  • Delete the app. Everything stored locally goes with it.

Decks you have bought stay tied to your Apple ID and come back through Restore purchases.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have rights to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection. Since we process no personal data about you, we have nothing to disclose and nothing to erase. Write to us anyway if you have questions, and you may complain to a data protection authority at any time.

Third-party data used in the app

Word selection, frequency ranks, gender and verb forms are based on Lexique 3.83 by Boris New and Christophe Pallier, licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0. The photographs on the deck covers come from Pexels. Both are content inside the app rather than services: nothing is sent to either.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new date appears at the top. As long as the app collects no data, there is not much here to change.
