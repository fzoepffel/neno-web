Néno

Néno teaches French vocabulary, forms and grammar. It works offline, keeps everything on your device, and asks for no account.

Getting help

Write to [[CONTACT EMAIL]](mailto:[CONTACT EMAIL]). Say which iPhone you have and which iOS version, and describe what you expected to happen. Mail in German, English or French is all fine.

Restoring a purchase

Settings in the app has Restore purchases, which asks the App Store what this Apple ID has already bought and unlocks it again. It needs a connection; nothing else in the app does.

Your progress

Everything you have learned is stored on your device. Settings can write it to a file and read it back, which is how progress moves to a new phone. Deleting the app deletes it.

Privacy

Néno collects nothing, sends nothing and contains no analytics. The full policy is [in English](privacy.html) and [auf Deutsch](datenschutz.html).
